Services

IPS Interior

Interior consulting services

  • We offer the customer several versions of products and budgets corresponding to the project
  • We advise on technical issues. If necessary, we also optimize the budget

Supply of interior materials

  • We offer budgeting and alternative versions of selected products to the customer
  • After selecting the products, we agree to the terms of the contract
  • In parallel with the order of materials with our partner company, we are on the line with the customer and, if desired, we also share recommendations on technical issues.

IPS Facade

Facade design

  • According to the rendering and drawings provided by the architect, our engineers prepare detailed nodes and technical solutions
  • During the engineering process of the project, we take into account climatic conditions – wind load, water penetration, temperature changes and others. Also, we pay attention to the features of materials and cost optimization
  • We supervise the work process so that all details are fulfilled according to the project

Supply of cladding materials

  • In accordance with the visual/architecture of the project, we offer the customer several versions of cladding materials.
  • We agree to the terms of delivery of the selected materials and the terms of the contract
  • We order materials from our partner company. We control that production and transportation happen on time

Installation of cladding materials

  • We perform installation services according to the agreed project
  • We have constant communication and reporting with the customer during the work process
  • The works are performed by our experienced and certified professionals. They use modern tools and technologies during the installation of the facade, which preserves the quality and durability of the facade
  • We have a 5-year warranty period for the work we have done

Supply of building materials

  • First, we check the details and requirements of the project to correctly define the needs
  • In accordance with the project, we offer the customer several materials  version and budget, as well as technical solutions
  • We agree to the terms of delivery and the terms of the contract
  • In the case of large projects , depending on the need, the representative of the company producing the selected material is involved , so that all technical details are correctly calculated.
  • After delivery of the products  Periodic monitoring is carried out by us to ensure that the products are stored and used correctly. Also, if desired, theoretical and practical knowledge is shared before the end of the project.
