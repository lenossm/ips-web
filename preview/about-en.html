About us

Mission and Vision

At IPS, we know that proper project planning depends on in-depth knowledge of processes and materials.

In fact, any construction product is just a material, which, regardless of quality and price, can be used correctly or incorrectly. The quality of the buildings is determined by the qualification and not only the price of the material.

At IPS, we start and finish every project with knowledge.

    The company IPS started its activity in the direction of interior in 2016

Team and Career

The main strength of IPS lies in its team. We carefully select our team members, and then carefully train, develop, and develop them into masters of their craft.

In the IPS team, we all share common values and this helps us execute projects flawlessly.

If our field of activity interests you and our values match your skills, then take a look at our vacancies or fill out the application form.

Social Responsibility

IPS’ corporate social responsibility includes promoting, supporting and disseminating knowledge related to construction, in particular, facade and interior finishing. In this direction, the company has a number of plans, which it will implement step by step.

The purpose of the company’s CSR activity is to contribute to the development and improvement of the industry and, in general, the environment in which it operates.
